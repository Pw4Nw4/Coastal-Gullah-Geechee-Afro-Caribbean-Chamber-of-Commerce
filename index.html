About the chamber

Our mission

We educate, empower and advocate for Gullah Geechee, Afro-Caribbean, minority-owned and women-owned businesses throughout the Georgia Coast and beyond.

Through culturally rooted business education, partnerships, visibility, advocacy and access to resources, we're building a business community that honors our heritage and opens doors for the next generation.

It began with one thought from our president, Mahogany Bowers: “I'm Gullah Geechee, and I don't see me.” The chamber makes sure the people who created this culture are seen in it, credited for it and able to build on it.

Get to know the chamber

Our four pillars

Business Incubator & Resource Pipeline

Culturally responsive education, mentorship, technical assistance, certifications and practical tools to help businesses launch, grow and thrive.

Cultural Tourism & Economic Equity

More visibility and opportunity for artisans, entrepreneurs, hospitality professionals and heritage-centered businesses.

Educational Assets & Land Preservation

Education and resources that support ownership, ancestral land preservation, cultural sustainability and long-term legacy.

Collective Economic Voice

Advocacy for Gullah Geechee, Afro-Caribbean, Black, Brown, minority-owned and women-owned businesses throughout the Georgia Coast and beyond.
