We educate, empower and advocate for Gullah Geechee, Afro-Caribbean, minority-owned and women-owned businesses throughout the Georgia Coast and beyond.

Through culturally rooted business education, partnerships, visibility, advocacy and access to resources, we're building a business community that honors our heritage and opens doors for the next generation.
